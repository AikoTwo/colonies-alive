# Colonies Alive

**An addon for [MineColonies](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/minecolonies) that brings your colony to life.**

Your citizens stop being little workers that walk from A to B. Children play and cry, go to school (or skip it), adults chat and fall in love, babies are born, crawl and get tucked into bed, and everybody has a mood you can *see*.

> 🇧🇷 Versão em português mais abaixo.

---

## ✨ Features

### 👧 Children
- **Play, alone or in groups**: tag, dancing, ring games (*ciranda*), patty-cake, hugging, whispering secrets, chasing and petting animals, picking flowers to give away, splashing in water, dancing to a jukebox.
- **Real emotions**: they cry with tears when hurt, giggle, pout with crossed arms, hold grudges and make friends.
- **Hit one, and it gets back at you**: a flurry of harmless little punches, then it wants an apology.
- **Expressive faces**: blinking eyelids, closed eyes when laughing, a cute bouncy walk.
- **Accessories**: hair bows, flower crowns, school backpacks, sleeping caps, Christmas hats in December, and colorful **band-aids that pile up the more hurt they are**.

### 🏫 School
- Class runs from 6:00 to 12:00. Pupils read, write, raise their hand and doze off, and the teacher gestures.
- Recess with calm games.
- Some pupils **skip school**. A parent or the teacher comes to fetch them and tells them off, and at home they stand in the corner, grounded until morning.

### 💕 Adults and social life
- Adults chat in their free time, become friends, become **rivals and argue**, and can **fall in love and become partners** (as real MineColonies partners).
- Citizens **start conversations with you** through MineColonies' own interaction window. There are four shuffled answers (kind, kind, uninterested, rude), and what you say changes how much they like you.
- A new **"Social life" tab** in the citizen window shows mood, affinity with you, partner, friends, rivals and family.

### 👶 Families and babies
- Couples decide to have a baby (or you encourage them). The **pregnancy shows**, even under armor, and lasts 3 in-game days.
- **Babies**: they sit and babble, crawl, try to stand up, wobble a few steps and plop down. They play peekaboo with older kids, wear onesies, pacifiers, bibs and rattles, and grow up after ~4 real hours.
- **Bedtime**: at night a parent or older sibling always comes to carry the baby to bed, **getting out of their own bed if needed**.
- **Healer visits**: a sick or hurt baby doesn't walk to the hospital. The healer comes to it.
- MineColonies' random births are turned off: babies come from your couples.

### 😢 Moods you can see
- Sad citizens **walk hunched, head hanging, slower**. Happy babies clap and beam, sad babies whimper.
- Guards don't get sad from fighting monsters. That's their job.

### 😠 Bullies (new in 0.1.0)
- Once in a while a **mercenary or a tavern adventurer hits a child or baby**. It's a real hit (reach, line of sight, knockback), and then they sneer and walk off.
- A **guard or relative who sees it starts a real fistfight** with them. The bully runs off when beaten.
- The child **runs crying to mum or dad to tell**, and the parent listens, gets furious, goes after the bully or gives a hug.
- A baby can't run: it **wails until the family comes, picks it up and rocks it**.
- Nobody dies from it.

## 📦 Requirements

| | |
|---|---|
| Minecraft | **1.21.1** |
| Loader | **NeoForge 21.1.265+** |
| **MineColonies** | **1.1.1411+** for 1.21.1 (*required*, from CurseForge), plus its own dependencies: Structurize, BlockUI, Domum Ornamentum, Multi-Piston |
| Side | **Client and server** (install on both) |

Works with shaders (tested with Iris + Complementary Reimagined).

## 🛠️ Commands (operators)
- `/coloniesalive` (looking at a citizen): mood, affinity, partner, friends.
- `/coloniesalive gravidez [%]`, `nascer`, `crescer`: pregnancy, birth, grow a baby up (testing).
- `/coloniesalive valentao` (looking at a child): a bully goes for it (testing).
- `/coloniesalive consertar`: fixes a stuck MineColonies raid.

## ⚠️ Notes
- Early version (beta). **Back up your world** before adding it to a long-running colony.
- `/coloniesalive adultos` turns **every** child into an adult and **can't be undone**.
- Languages: English, Português (Brasil).

# 🇧🇷 Colonies Alive (Português)

**Um addon para o [MineColonies](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/minecolonies) que dá vida à sua colônia.**

Os colonos deixam de ser trabalhadores que andam do ponto A ao B. As crianças brincam e choram e vão à escola (ou matam aula). Os adultos conversam e se apaixonam. Bebês nascem, engatinham e são levados para a cama, e todo mundo tem um humor que dá para *ver*.

### Destaques
- **Crianças**: pega-pega, dança, ciranda, palminhas, abraços, segredinhos, bichinhos, flores, água e dança na jukebox. Choram com lágrimas, dão risadinhas, fazem bico, guardam mágoa, fazem amizades e se vingam com socos fofinhos e sem dano.
- **Escola**: aula das 6h às 12h, com poses de leitura, escrita, mão levantada e cochilo, além de recreio. Quem mata aula é buscado pelos pais ou professor e fica de castigo no canto.
- **Vida social**: amizades, rivalidades, brigas, namoro e casamento. Os colonos puxam assunto com você pela janela do MineColonies, e há uma **aba "Vida social"** nova.
- **Família**: gravidez visível (até de armadura), bebês que engatinham, tentam andar, brincam de "cadê? achou!" e são levados à cama por um parente, que **levanta da própria cama para isso**. O curandeiro vem até o bebê doente.
- **Humor visível**: colono triste anda curvado e de cabeça baixa. Bebê feliz bate palmas, bebê triste choraminga. Guardas não ficam tristes por apanhar de monstros.
- **Valentões**: às vezes um mercenário ou aventureiro bate numa criança. Um guarda ou parente que vê **briga de verdade** com ele, a criança **corre chorando contar para a mãe**, e o bebê **berra até alguém pegar no colo**. Ninguém morre.

### Requisitos
Minecraft **1.21.1**, NeoForge **21.1.265+** e **MineColonies 1.1.1411+** (obrigatório, do CurseForge), com as dependências dele. Instale no **cliente e no servidor**. Funciona com shaders.

### Avisos
Versão beta: **faça backup do mundo**. O comando `/coloniesalive adultos` é **irreversível**.
