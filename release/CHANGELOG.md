# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — first public release (beta)
*Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1.265+ · MineColonies 1.1.1411+*

### Children
- Solo and group games: tag, dancing, ciranda, patty-cake, hugs, whispering, animals, flowers, water, jukebox dancing, peekaboo with babies.
- Crying with tears and sobs, giggles, pouting, grudges, friendships.
- Harmless revenge on a player who hits them, followed by an apology topic.
- Blinking eyelids, laughing faces, bouncy walk.
- Accessories: bows, flower crowns, backpacks (mornings only), sleeping caps, Christmas hats (December), band-aids depending on health.

### School
- Class 6:00–12:00 with class poses, recess games and a gesturing teacher.
- Skipping school: fetched by a parent or the teacher, told off, sent to the corner and grounded.

### Social life
- Adult chats, friendships, rivalries, arguments, romance and partners.
- Citizens start conversations through MineColonies' interaction window, and your answer (kind / uninterested / rude) changes affinity and mood.
- New "Social life" tab in the citizen window.

### Families
- Pregnancy (3 in-game days, visible belly, also under armor), birth, babies (~4 real hours until they grow up).
- Babies sit, babble, crawl, try to walk, wear onesies and accessories, and get carried to bed at night.
- Bedtime: a relative **always** comes for the baby, even getting out of their own bed, and goes back to sleep afterwards.
- The healer visits sick or hurt babies.
- MineColonies' random births are disabled; the starting citizens are untouched.

### Moods
- Gloomy citizens walk hunched and slower.
- Babies show their mood: happy ones clap and beam, sad ones whimper with a few tears.
- Guards (knights, archers, druids…) no longer get sad from being hit by monsters (hits from players still count).
- Fixed: citizens could keep the hunched "sad" walk while their mood was already happy.

### Bullies
- Mercenaries and tavern adventurers occasionally hit a nearby child or baby with a real hit (reach, line of sight, knockback). They are rare.
- Guards and relatives who see (or hear) it start a real fistfight with the bully. The bully runs off once beaten. Nobody dies.
- The child cries, runs to mum, dad or an older sibling to tell, and gets hugged. The parent goes after the bully if it is still around.
- Babies wail until a relative picks them up and rocks them.
- Children who were just bullied aren't dragged to school mid-scene.

### Commands (operators)
- `/coloniesalive`, `gravidez`, `nascer`, `crescer`, `valentao`, `consertar`, `caminhos`, `adultos` (irreversible).

### Compatibility
- Tested with Iris 1.8.14 + Sodium 0.8.13 + Complementary Reimagined r5.9.3.
